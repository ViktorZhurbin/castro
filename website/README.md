## RULES

The satirical voice earns its place when it reframes a technical constraint in a way that makes the constraint more memorable, not just more decorated. It should do cognitive work, not just aesthetic work.

**Satire as a wrapper.** Jokes go in proper nouns, section titles, and illustrations; the explanatory prose underneath stays plain and technical. Keeping them separable is what lets the explanation stay clear.

**Where the voice lives.** The landing page and `404` carry it. `docs/` is usage-only and stays plain informational (see `CLAUDE.md`).

**The design has to commit.** See `DESIGN.md`. Muddiness and SaaS-ness are the failure mode — a half-hearted constructivist poster breaks the joke rather than softening it.

**`public/space-castro.jpg`** is a reference for the register, not site art. It's a reminder to consider illustration in future work; don't wire it into a page as-is.
